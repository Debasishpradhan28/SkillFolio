Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. All core features including resume generation and the live alchemist playground work completely anonymously without accounts or login walls.

How does the Resume QR code work?

When you export your resume to PDF, our generator auto-compiles a tracking QR code pointing directly to your deployed portfolio web address.

Are the resumes truly ATS-compliant?

Yes. Our Harvard and Tech layouts strictly avoid multi-column tables, hidden text layers, or unsupported graphical elements that confuse parsing bots.

What is Roast Mode?

Roast Mode passes your resume data through an aggressive persona model designed to mimic top-tier engineering managers.
